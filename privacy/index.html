Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RANNTA handles information in connection with its websites, blockchain infrastructure, and public RPC services.

RANNTA X-Chain Public RPC

The RANNTA X-Chain public RPC endpoint is available at https://rpc.rannta.com.

RANNTA does not use the public RPC endpoint for advertising tracking or third-party analytics. RANNTA does not intentionally correlate wallet addresses or blockchain RPC requests with individual user profiles.

Blockchain Data

Blockchain transactions and related data submitted to or retrieved from RANNTA X-Chain may become part of a public blockchain record. Public blockchain data is inherently visible to network participants and may be retained permanently as part of the chain history.

Website Information

RANNTA websites may process ordinary technical information required to deliver pages and protect service availability, such as browser, device, network, and request information. This information is not used by RANNTA to build advertising profiles.

Cookies and Analytics

RANNTA does not use the public RPC endpoint for advertising cookies or third-party behavioral analytics. Separate website pages may use essential technologies required for normal site operation.

Third-Party Services

Links or integrations may direct users to independent third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies and are not controlled by RANNTA.

Security and Service Protection

RANNTA may apply technical measures necessary to protect infrastructure, prevent abuse, maintain availability, and investigate operational or security incidents.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as RANNTA services evolve. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy or infrastructure questions, visit RANNTA Contact or the main website at rannta.com.
