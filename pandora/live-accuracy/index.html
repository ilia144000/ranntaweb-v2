What is being predicted?

Accuracy compares up to 434 eligible settlements per horizon. The baseline selects the most common class among the last 10 eligible prior outcomes, using the most recent outcome for ties. Official horizons allow the full settlement window before counting an outcome as known and use a no-change baseline until 10 outcomes are available. The public mirror publishes in batches. Some receipts first appear after settlement.

Public results, forecast probabilities, and issuance / settlement timestamps are available in the verified receipt history above.
