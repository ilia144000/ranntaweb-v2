What RANNTA Core Provides

  • Independent RANNTA X-Chain Mainnet node identity.
  • Full Mainnet synchronization and local chain-state tracking.
  • Live node health, peer, RPC and synchronization status.
  • Secure public-node TLS enrollment with unique per-install credentials.
  • Reward participation telemetry and challenge verification.
  • Direct reward-wallet binding through MetaMask and compatible EIP-1193 EVM wallets.
  • No seed phrase or wallet private key is requested or stored by the reward system.

Node Rewards — Shadow Mode

Node Rewards are currently operating in Shadow Mode for validation and measurement. No real RNTX rewards are being distributed at this stage.

Current qualification testing measures uptime, synchronization health, challenge success, node identity and reward-wallet verification. The current qualification target is at least 6 qualified hours per day for at least 20 qualified days in a month.

A fully qualified node has a modeled minimum monthly reward of 0.5 RNTX under the current Shadow policy, with healthy-node targets generally modeled above that floor. These values are test-policy parameters and do not represent an active payment promise while Shadow Mode remains enabled.
