What RANNTA Core Provides

  • Independent RANNTA X-Chain Mainnet node identity.
  • Full Mainnet synchronization and local chain-state tracking.
  • Live node health, peer, RPC and synchronization status.
  • HybridRequired public-node transport using X25519 + ML-KEM-768 + HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM.
  • Encrypted chain-sync records and authenticated peer sessions.
  • Reward participation telemetry and challenge verification.
  • Direct reward-wallet binding through MetaMask and compatible EIP-1193 EVM wallets.
  • No seed phrase or wallet private key is requested or stored by the reward system.

Node Rewards - Measured Participation

Qualified participation is recorded from the time a node enters the Node Rewards measurement system. Uptime, synchronization health, challenge performance, node identity and verified reward-wallet status are used to determine eligibility.

The current qualification target is at least 6 qualified hours per day for at least 20 qualified days in a month, together with healthy synchronization and challenge performance.

A fully qualified node has a current minimum qualified reward target of 0.5 RNTX per monthly settlement period. Healthy-node targets are generally 0.8-1.2 RNTX, subject to the published scoring, anti-fraud and cap rules.

The accounting system is currently operating in Shadow Mode: qualification and reward amounts are measured and recorded, while on-chain transfers remain under owner-controlled manual approval. Eligible early-node participation recorded during this measurement period is intended to be included in the reward settlement process when manual RNTX distribution is activated.
