RANNTA Entity

RANNTA is one ecosystem with distinct but connected layers: the official RANNTA token on The Open Network (TON), the live RANNTA X-Change, and the independent RANNTA X-Chain blockchain network.

RANNTA X-Chain

RANNTA X-Chain is an implemented independent blockchain network and interoperability layer. It is not a TON application, TON middleware layer, TON Jetton extension, or merely an emerging architecture.
