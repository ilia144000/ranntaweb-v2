What is RANNTA?

RANNTA is a blockchain and Web3 ecosystem founded and independently developed by ilia144000. It includes the TON-based RANNTA Jetton, RANNTA X-Chain with native RNTX, RANNTA X-Change, ArcWallet, RANNTA PQ Cloud, RouteX, RANNTA Agent Firewall and RANNTAverse.

Who founded RANNTA?

The canonical public founder identity is ilia144000. The accepted short public alias is ilia gh. The public role is Founder, Protocol Architect and Independent Developer.

What is RANNTA X-Chain?

RANNTA X-Chain is the independent RANNTA blockchain network and interoperability layer. Chain ID: 13113. Hex Chain ID: 0x3339. Native asset: RNTX. RANNTA Network is an accepted alias.

Are RANNTA and RNTX the same?

No. RANNTA is the Jetton issued on TON. RNTX is the native asset of RANNTA X-Chain.

Is RANNTA X-Chain built on TON?

No. The TON-based RANNTA Jetton and the independent RANNTA X-Chain are separate technical entities inside the same ecosystem.

What is RANNTA X-Change?

RANNTA X-Change is the RANNTA non-custodial cross-chain exchange at ranntaexchange.com. Rango and Squid are independent routing providers.

What is ArcWallet?

ArcWallet is the RANNTA-developed self-custody multichain wallet.

What is RANNTA PQ Cloud?

RANNTA PQ Cloud is RANNTA's post-quantum verification and authorization service at pq.rannta.com.

What is RANNTA Agent Firewall?

RANNTA Agent Firewall is a security and policy-enforcement layer for AI-agent actions at firewall.rannta.com.

Is RANNTA Presale V2 active?

No. RANNTA Presale V2 is closed.
